# ⚖️ LegalPath

> AI-powered open-source legal research and workflow platform for students, researchers, lawyers, and legal innovators.

## Overview

LegalPath is an open-source LegalTech platform designed to make legal research, document analysis, and knowledge discovery more accessible through modern AI technologies.

Instead of replacing legal professionals, LegalPath helps them work faster by organizing information, summarizing complex legal texts, and providing intelligent research assistance.

## Features

* 🤖 AI-assisted legal research
* 📚 Document summarization
* 🔍 Semantic search across legal sources
* 📝 Case and legislation analysis
* 🌍 Multi-language support (planned)
* 🔌 Extensible API architecture
* 💻 Modern and developer-friendly codebase

## Why LegalPath?

Legal information is often fragmented, difficult to navigate, and time-consuming to analyze.

LegalPath aims to provide an open and collaborative platform where developers and legal professionals can build tools that improve access to legal knowledge and increase productivity.

## Tech Stack

* React

* Node.js
* Python
* AI APIs
* MongoDB / PostgreSQL (depending on deployment)
